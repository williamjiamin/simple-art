Privacy: the Simple Art app

Last updated 2026-10-21. This page covers the Simple Art guided-viewing app (by MasterAllArts) for iPhone and iPad. The masterallarts.com website, with its accounts and course subscriptions, is a separate service.

The short version

The app does not ask you to sign in, does not track you, has no advertising, and does not run analytics. It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

What the app downloads

To show a guided viewing, the app downloads artwork images, story scripts, narration audio, music and sound effects from our content server. These are ordinary file downloads, the same as loading a web page: your device's network address is necessarily part of each request. The app sends nothing else about you and we do not use these requests to identify or profile you.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, such as narration language, reduced motion, and music and sound effects on or off.
  • A cache of downloaded content so stories play offline. You can clear it in Settings, and it is removed when you delete the app.
  • Anything you draw in the Studio and practice exercises. It is not uploaded.
  • Your favourites, viewing streak, course progress and offline downloads.
  • The optional morning reminder: a local notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • The Home Screen widget, which only shows the day's painting from the app's own cache.

Saving to Photos

If you choose to save a painting, narration film or share card, the app asks for add-only access to your photo library. It can only add the item you chose; it never reads, browses or uploads your photos.

Purchases

Simple Art Premium (monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime unlock) is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card, name or Apple ID. The app asks the App Store on your device whether you have an active purchase, and that answer stays on your device. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings. Purchases are governed by Apple's standard licence agreement.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this changes, we will update this page and the date above before the app behaves differently. Questions: email support@masterallarts.com.

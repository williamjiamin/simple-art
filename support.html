Simple Art support

Questions, problems or a painting you would like us to add? Email support@masterallarts.com. We usually answer within two working days.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel or change a subscription

Settings in the app → Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Premium until the end of the paid period.

Narration language

Every guided viewing is narrated in English and Japanese. Choose the language in the app's Settings.

Nothing loads

Stories stream from content.masterallarts.com and are cached for offline use. Check your connection, then pull to refresh the library.

Simple Art

Art History Guide for iPhone and iPad

Look at paintings the way a teacher would show them to you. Guided viewings of 148 public-domain masterpieces, from Vermeer and Rembrandt to Hokusai, each narrated in English and Japanese, with the camera moving to exactly what the narration describes.

What's inside

  • Guided viewings: narration with word-by-word captions and highlights on the painting
  • Explore: full-screen, pinch into every brushstroke
  • Colour, value and depth views that show how the picture is built
  • Studio: practise palette, values and mark direction after the masters

Price

Free to download, with 10 masterpieces free. Simple Art Premium unlocks the whole collection: monthly, yearly (7 days free) or a one-time lifetime unlock.

Artwork images are public domain or CC0, from the National Gallery of Art, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago and the Rijksmuseum.
